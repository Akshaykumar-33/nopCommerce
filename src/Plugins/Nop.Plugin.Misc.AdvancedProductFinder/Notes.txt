Promotional note about Advanced Product Finder plugin for nopCommerce
===========

Advanced Product Finder adds a dedicated multi-filter discovery page with:
- keyword, category, manufacturer, price, and specification filters
- color swatches for specification options that define ColorSquaresRgb
- AJAX product results with paging
- recently viewed products
- complete-the-set suggestions from related products or the same category
- optional homepage CTA widget
